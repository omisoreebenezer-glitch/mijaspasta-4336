# Mija's Pasta — Design System

## Brand
Premium Nigerian pasta restaurant in Osogbo. Top-tier urban food brand energy — youthful, luxury, addictive.

## Palette
- Background: `#0a0a0a` (matte black), `#111111` (deep charcoal)
- Gold accent: `#D4AF37` / `#C9A227`
- Orange highlight: `#E8671A` / `#FF7A22`
- White text: `#F5F5F5`
- Muted text: `#9B9B9B`
- Glass card: `rgba(255,255,255,0.05)` with `backdrop-blur`
- Gold glow: `0 0 20px rgba(212,175,55,0.4)`

## Typography
- Display/Hero: `Bebas Neue` — all caps, massive
- Headings: `Montserrat` — bold 700/900
- Body: `Poppins` — 400/500

## Motion
- Page load: staggered fade+slide up reveals
- Scroll: IntersectionObserver class toggle for `.reveal`
- Hover: scale, glow, blur lift
- Hero: parallax + floating particles
- Loading screen: spaghetti strand draw animation

## Layout
- Fullscreen hero
- Asymmetric sections
- Glassmorphism cards
- Overlapping elements
- Bold oversized type

## Feel
Cinematic. Premium. Hungry.
